PENG DUMPLINGS
THE RESTAURANT WITH HOMEMADE DUMPLINGS IN BASEL
Welcome to PENG DUMPLINGS, located in the heart of the Gundeli district in Basel. Discover our diverse selection of homemade dumplings and much more! Depending on your taste and appetite, you can mix and match our dishes and even share them with a group. In the evening, we treat you to our à la carte menu, and at lunchtime we offer a selection of fresh lunch specials as well as a selection from our evening menu. In the evening, our bar also invites you to enjoy a drink before or after your meal. We look forward to your visit!
Of course, you can also order our food and drinks for pickup directly online for both lunch and dinner.

DUMPLINGS
Dumplings 餃子 [jiǎozi] are small chinese dough wrapped fillings of meat, fish and vegetables which are made in different forms. In China dumplings are typically served at tea time and are part of a variation of small bite-sized portioned meals – Dim Sum – which translates as “Touch of the Heart”.
PENG
The hybrid creature peng 鵬 [péng] from the chinese mythology is the eponym of our restaurant. As this chimera merges fish and bird, likewise we combine all kind of ingredients into a variation of delicious dumplings.
PHILOSOPHY
Our gastronomic philosophy is to reinterpret this classic chinese treat with influences of the international cuisine. Whenever possible we use regional and biological products. In the interest of sustainability and the variety of taste, our handmade dumplings are prepared based on seasonal produce.
