Green Gorilla Café
Green Gorilla originated from a simple idea : giving the best possible food to the community we love. So we set out to design a collection of superfood that would answer your needs. Whether it‘s recharging your immune system, helping your heart, or boosting your digestion, we use the best that nature has to offer in a variety of ways to enhance your day to day life.
Quality comes first at Green Gorilla, and that won‘t ever change. The food and beverages we serve here are the result of making sure we work with the best producers, using only the best techniques, so that you only get the best.
