WINE BAR & PINTXOS
COR (meaning "heart" in Catalan) embodies a deep love for savoring life's best moments—enjoying great wine, delicious food, and, most importantly, wonderful company. We put our heart into everything we do, carefully curating the finest biodynamic wines, tapas, and pintxos to bring you the richness and diversity of Spanish culture—right in the heart of Zurich.
We pour our heart into all that we do, curating the best biodynamic wines, tapas and pintxos for you to experience the richness and diversity of Spanish culture in the heart of Zurich.
ETHICALLY SOURCED
We bring the focus on small, ethical producers and farmers — and a commitment to serving great wine and food.
SUSTAINABLE AND SEASONAL
Our organic produce is sourced a curated selection of sustainable and regenerative farms. Our menu changes together with the seasons.
CELEBRATING DIVERSITY
From menu building to wine sourcing, we celebrate cultural and gender diversity helping our visitors to experience food and wine at its best.
Our mission is to bring the wine world closer to our guests and, in doing so, bring our guests together.
This year we are excited to launch the Lunar Wines series, dedicated to honoring the women who are driving change in the world of natural and biodynamic winemaking.
