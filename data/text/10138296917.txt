UMAMIDO GRANCY
Located in the vibrant Grancy neighbourhood, just steps from the CFF train station in Lausanne, Umamido Grancy is thrilled to bring our authentic ramen to the Vaud region. Open every day from 11:30 AM to 10:00 PM, we invite you to enjoy the simple pleasures of our carefully prepared broths, tender noodles, and well-seasoned toppings. Our dishes emphasize traditional techniques and fresh ingredients, ensuring a delightful dining experience right in the bustling heart of Lausanne. Whether you’re looking for a quick lunch or a relaxing dinner, Umamido Grancy offers a warm welcome and genuine Japanese flavors. Stop by and discover your new favorite ramen spot!
