OMAKASE
At our core, we believe in quality over quantity. By mastering traditional techniques, we enhance each ingredient’s natural flavors, culminating in the pure essence of umami. Our Edo-style Omakase is a meticulously curated sequence of classic nigiri sushi and kaiseki dishes, celebrating the artistry of Japanese cuisine.
"Omakase" translates to "I leave it up to you", entrusting the chef to craft a menu based on the finest seasonal ingredients. Each course is prepared before your eyes and personally presented by the chef, creating an intimate and immersive dining experience.
We’re inviting you to this unique experience.
