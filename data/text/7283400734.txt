An industrial adventure
As we welcome you into our restaurant, we have the opportunity to help you discover, or rediscover, the most incredible story of Vevey's industrial past. Now restored after remaining abandoned for nearly 25 years, the "halle inox" (meaning the "stainless steel workshop") embodies nearly a century of history.
