Learn more about us
Welcome India King is situated on the Interlaken west train station and bank of the river, with it’s excellent food. Interlaken is an ideal place for the restaurant's exclusive Vegetarian and Non Vegetarian, Halal meat Indian Cuisine ranging from tandoori, various salads, Rotis to Biryanis. The Welcome India King offers you a gastronomical experience like never before!
This restaurant is the house of authentic Punjabi Indian cuisine and has been successful in satisfying customers with its delectable dishes. Known especially for the Snacks, Curry and Biryani`s, Welcome India King also serves other Indian dishes. The dishes are moderately priced, which gives you all the more reason to visit over and over again.
The Welcome India King is located 2 minutes away from the west train station and public parking facility opposite to the Restaurant. We can also be visited by post bus.
