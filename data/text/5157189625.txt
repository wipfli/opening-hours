ABOUT ZA ZAA
"When you have more than you need, build a longer table not a higher fence"
Is the motto and philosophy of our restaurant with Lebanese cuisine. We also have every reason to expand the tables; because our specialties are different, hot and cold, vegetarian, or vegan mezzes, which we serve in different variations according to the principle of "sharing is caring". We offer varied, matching, lovingly and freshly prepared drinks to the dishes served with warm pita bread.
MONDAY TO SATURDAY 11AM - 11PM. Hot meals troughout the day.
