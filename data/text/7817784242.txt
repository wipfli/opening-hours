Welcome to Top of Breakfast Interlaken
The best breakfast in Interlaken
Available all day, every day.
You’ll love starting your day with our exceptional breakfast menu. From mouthwatering egg-filled breakfast burritos, avocado toast or heavenly grilled halloumi and fried egg sandwiches, we’ve got an array of tasty options to choose from. Don’t miss our cream cheese and soft, scrambled egg bagel, our super-healthy homemade, sugar-free granola, and sweet, soft pancakes with mascarpone cream. Our lunch menu is packed with fresh, healthy food with plenty of vegan options.
Breakfast starts at 8 am and goes all day. Lunch starts at midday.  See you there!
Tasty & healthy!
We create all the breakfast food you crave using vegan and vegetarian stuff
Are you looking for a breakfast that sets you up for the day or helps reset you after a big night out, without compromising your desire to look after yourself. You’ve found it.
We use plant-based ingredients to create the best breakfast that you deserve.
Food
We make planet food
Welcome to our vegetarian and vegan cafe! We love baking and making everything in-house, and we’re excited to share our delicious creations with you for breakfast, brunch, or lunch. Did you know that choosing plant-based options is a great way to care for our planet? Let’s do our part to create a more sustainable future!

Drinks
The best coffee in town!
Come on in to our cosy cafe and treat yourself to our delicious Italian, espresso-based coffee. We’ve got oat and soya milk, so you can happily enjoy your favourite drink, knowing it’s good for your tummy and our planet. We have finely blended Chai tea to make delicious Chai lattes, and our Matcha powder is ceremonial-grade. Our hot & cold drink selection has been refined over the years to ensure your Velo Cafe experience is just as it should be.
