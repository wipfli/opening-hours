WELCOME
“Between old stone walls and a seasonal kitchen, a piece of Winterthur, the way it has always been.”
On Schulgasse 1, in the heart of Winterthur's old town, stands a house where generations before us have eaten, laughed and clinked glasses. The Obergass is a restaurant in the truest sense: grown over time, well kept, honest.
Inside, a cosy dining room awaits you, in front of it the Boulevard Café, and on the first floor the Obergass hall and a quiet terrace. We look forward to having you.
A GLIMPSE OF OBERGASS
Our house in motion
Take two and a half minutes with us: a tour of our four worlds under one roof, the craft behind our kitchen and the warm hospitality that Karin and Daniel Hunold have stood for since 1997.
