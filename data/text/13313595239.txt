LE CERCLE
Kitchen
A dining experience rooted in rhythm, seasonality and a deep respect for origin. Under the direction of Chef Matteo Salas, Le Cercle offers a refined yet approachable menu where each dish is inspired by the land and sourced within a 180km radius of Martigny. Celebrating simplicity, locality and creativity. The kitchen moves with the seasons to craft plates that feel both grounded and surprising. Every detail honours the ingredients, the place and the people they’re made for.

Welcome to Quartier LôDzè — a new kind of neighbourhood in the heart of the Swiss Alps. Nestled in Martigny, LôDzè is rooted in community and built on a foundation of thoughtful design, creativity and shared experiences. It’s a place where people come together to eat, rest, connect and restore. It’s a neighbourhood shaped by those who pass through it.
