OUR STORY
LEARN MORE ABOUT HOW WE BEGAN
Brasserie Uno is a rustic, casual fine dining restaurant in Zermatt Village. It was named after the owner’s grandfather, Uno, and was named ‘Brasserie’ (which means ‘brewery’ in French) as a nod to Uno’s time working in a brewery in Sweden. The warm, inviting interior features many personal items from Uno himself, including the Axevalla Pilsner sign from the brewery where he worked in the 1950s, his typewriter, skis, clock and more.
The Brasserie Uno collaborative kitchen is helmed by our talented head chef Luis Romo, along with chef Tommaso Guardascione, who create international dishes that come alive with fresh flavours and inventive ideas. We only use the finest ingredients that are regional and seasonal when possible, and in the warmer months we use produce grown in our gardens or forage wild ingredients from the local forest in our beautiful village to add to our dishes. We use bio (organic) products when available and our team, including Luis, Tommaso and owner Andreas Alm, are proud to offer inspiring vegetarian dishes alongside Swiss meat and fish dishes. We are a minimal food-waste restaurant and any food waste that is incurred is recycled into energy that powers the village.

WHO IS UNO?
DISCOVER THE MAN WHO INSPIRED BRASSERIE UNO
During a road trip through Sweden and Norway in the summer of 2018, I was thinking about what to call the restaurant. I’d had many ideas but none of them seemed right, then one day the name came to me: Brasserie Uno. Uno was the name of my grandfather and he’d worked in a brewery (brasserie means brewery), so naming the restaurant after him just felt right, even though the restaurant is not a French brasserie in any way.

ABOUT OUR MENU…
Our surprise small-plate degustation menu changes with the seasons and availability of products, which is why you won’t find our menu online. We use regional and seasonal products when possible and always use the highest-quality ingredients from trusted producers. We are a minimum food waste restaurant and believe in environmental and human sustainability.
