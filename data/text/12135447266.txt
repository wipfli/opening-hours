Restaurant
Restaurant Mühleye – relaxing by the pool
Restaurant Mühleye is THE place to be. Whether you want to treat yourself before swimming, during a long day at the pool, or after a walk nearby, you’ll always find the right refreshment or the coolest drink at Restaurant Mühleye!
Fresh, healthy and sometimes even homemade dishes are our focus.
From now on, we have a delicious selection of pizzas, lovingly prepared by our Italian chefs and straight from the original Italian pizza oven.
Enjoy a perfect summer day with fresh salads, frappés and coups – combined with an Aperol Spritz or an ice-cold Somersby!

Raclette, Paella & Grill
Due to limited seating, we recommend making a reservation! Table Reservation
Raclette on Monday
Every Monday, we serve raclette with delicious Valais Alpine cheese, accompanied by a smooth wine from the neighboring vineyards. Our raclette evenings are very popular and atmospheric, often with live music that makes the cheese melt even faster.
Paella every Thursday night!
The paella is prepared according to a classic recipe by our cook Willi from Spain. The fragrant scent of the south, with hot samba rhythms and a glass of sangria, makes for a great evening by the pool.
Sommer-Grill!
On those lovely days by the pool, we fire up the grill!
The menu features a variety of sausages and meats, served with a side of salad and/or fries.
Parties and Groups
We warmly welcome groups.
We are happy to welcome smaller groups in the restaurant, in the lively atmosphere of the swimming pool.
We are well-equipped for larger groups and offer ideal conditions right by the pool.
The infrastructure includes a grill, bar, marquees, picnic tables, and modern music systems. We design the food offer flexibly and individually according to your wishes – from simple snacks to complete barbecue menus.
Whether it’s a club event, company celebration, birthday, or school group, you can enjoy relaxing hours in a unique bathing atmosphere. We would be happy to advise you personally and put together a suitable offer for your group.
Kiosk Take Away
The kiosk is almost the most important thing in a swimming pool, apart from the water in the pool. At our kiosk, you can get everything you need for a successful day at the pool: e.g. fries, chicken nuggets, hot dogs, pizza, drinks, sweets and of course ICE CREAM!
LOOP
With Loop you can order food right from the lawn or the slide. When your order is ready, you’ll receive a text message. It’s that simple—and no waiting in line.
SWIMMING PASS
You can add credit to your swimming pass at the ticket office, and it can then be used as a form of payment at the kiosk and restaurant.
ZERO WASTE with reusable tableware
At the KIOSK, we prefer reCIRCLE reusable dishes.
Deposit for the reCIRCLE PizzaBOX and plates CHF 10.- and for the reCIRCLE cup CHF 5.-
After eating, return your reCIRCLE product and get your money back, or wash the reCIRCLE product yourself and have it refilled again and again. It’s that simple!
At the new washing station, you can wash your dishes yourself for the next time.
Let’s reduce waste and protect the environment together!
