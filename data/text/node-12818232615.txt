BORDERLESS KITCHEN POPUP
Pop up – limited time !
On April 2nd, 2025, we’re bringing the vibrant flavors of Asia to Zurich with the launch of Borderless Kitchen Street Food Bar in the Dar Cocktail Bar.
Expect Asian food like you’ve never tasted before – inspired by the bustling streets of Asia. We’re talking fresh, vibrant, and utterly delicious – all made in-house with love! From bold flavours to comforting classics, every bite is a journey. And to top it off, refreshing cocktails and drinks (also non-alcoholic) that pair perfectly with the food. Mark your calendars and join us for a taste of something extraordinary, all vegan.
See you there! Come hungry, leave happy.
