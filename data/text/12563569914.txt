Burrito Bandito - street food from Los Angeles
Los Angeles streetfood with swiss products
A Californian in Bern who craves the healthy, energetic street food from his homeland. What could be more natural than bringing the stimulating tastes and enticing smells of Californian-Mexican cuisine to Switzerland? That's exactly what Sam Sager, founder and owner of Burrito Bandito, has done.
Burrito Bandito promises true California flavor thanks to Sam's own recipe creations. But between "bombass" beans, rice, cheese and meat, there's a lot more. For example, Burrito Bandito uses vegan alternatives, meat from the local butcher and the complete absence of additives in our burritos.

Festivals & Events
With our food truck and food stand we were, among others, at the Gurtenfestival, the Montreux Jazz Festival, the Royal Arena and at the Eidgenössischen Schwingfest. In addition, there are many smaller, local events in Bern that we continue to take part in yearly.
