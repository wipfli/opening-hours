We use fresh ingredients for our delicious dishes
We prioritize freshness and quality; every dish we serve is crafted with the freshest ingredients, guaranteeing an unforgettable dining experience.
Philosophy
Osha Thai has its roots firmly embedded in the rich culinary traditions of Thailand. With a deep respect for authenticity, innovative cooking techniques, and a flare for presentation, we aim to bring Thailand’s vibrant street food culture right here to Luzern.
How you find us
You will find us in a location that is easily accessible by public transport, about 100 meters from the "Hornbach". Public transport users take the train to "Littau" or the bus to "Säntihof". Then it is only a few minutes' walk. Drivers can park right in front of our restaurant.
