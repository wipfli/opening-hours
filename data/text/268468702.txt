yume ramen has holidays until 09.08.
yume ramen Limmatplatz will transform into an Okonomiyaki Pop Up inspired by Osakas Streetfood culture from 12.05. till End of December .
We serve a new concept with Osaka style Okonomiyaki, a savory Japanese pancake, along with new starters, new Desserts and a fresh selection of highballs and cocktails.
We are open daily for lunch and dinner.
Reservations are welcome and walk in are also accepted.
yume-ramen offers 100% homemade Ramen.
Broth and noodles are prepared carefully everyday at the store.
Join us and enjoy the real taste and the spirit of Japan!
