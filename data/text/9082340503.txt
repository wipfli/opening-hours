Welcome to SHIN – The Art of Asian Fusion Dining
At SHIN, we curate a dining experience that is intimate, elegant, and unforgettable—a true feast for the senses. Time and space are transformed, making every moment one to treasure.
Our approach to Asian fusion cuisine blends tradition with innovation, offering a refined yet relaxed atmosphere. Seated in high walnut handcrafted chairs, you’ll be immersed in a journey where each dish is presented with care, every flavor thoughtfully layered. Whether you're new to the experience or a dedicated food enthusiast, you will leave with a sense of euphoria.
Seafood lies at the heart of our culinary expression. From pristine sashimi to delicately steamed shellfish and flame-seared coastal treasures, each course celebrates the purity and depth of oceanic flavors. Between courses, we reimagine traditional Asian notes, using vibrant seafood as our muse.
With ingredients sourced both domestically and through exclusive international partners, our chef curates a dynamic tasting menu that evolves daily—honoring authenticity while embracing creativity.
The Omakase-inspired menu is shaped by the finest seasonal offerings, hand-selected from sustainable and wild-harvested sources. Every dish tells a story, blending time-honored techniques with modern artistry.
At SHIN, we believe dining is more than a meal—it’s an experience. Every bite, every aroma, and every moment is crafted to be cherished.
