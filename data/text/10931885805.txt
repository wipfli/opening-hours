Discover the Authentic Taste of South India
Welcome to Masal Dosa, your ultimate destination for Genuine South Indian culinary delights, where every bite brings you the Authentic flavors of Chettinad, South India. We strive to bring you the rich, flavorful tradition of South Indian (CHETTINAD) recipies straight from traditional kitchens of Chettinad region.
Our restaurant specializes in Dosas, a popular South Indian delicacy made from fermented rice and lentil batter. Whether you’re a long-time fan of South Indian food or a curious newcomer, we promise an authentic culinary experience that will leave you craving for more.
Our Menu
Signature Dosas:
Try our best-selling dosas like the  Authentic Masal Dosa, Crispy Ghee Roast Dosa and Savory Podi Dosa.
Specialty Dosas:
Indulge in our specialty dosas such as the Cheesee Dosa, Minced meat Dosa (Chicken or Lamb) – also know as
Kari Dosa (Kari – Tamil word for Meat, which is different from Curry – Sauce) , and Chocolate Dosa for a unique twist.
Accompaniments:
Enjoy your dosa with our delicious chutneys and sambar, made fresh daily.
South Indian Delights:
In addition to dosas, we offer a range of South Indian dishes including Idli, Vada, and Uttapam.
Week-end Specials:
We also have special menus such as Vegeterian and Non-Vegetarian Thalis and traditionally cooked
Chicken or Mutton Briyani on Saturdays and Sundays

Why Choose Masal Dosa?
Authentic South Indian Recipes:
Our dosas are prepared using traditional recipes passed down through generations, ensuring an authentic taste that is
both delicious and satisfying.
Fresh Ingredients:
We use only the freshest ingredients to prepare our dishes, ensuring a delightful and healthy dining experience.
Variety of Dosas:
From the classic Masala Dosa and Plain Dosa to innovative creations like Cheese Dosa and Chocolate Dosa, our menu has

Vegan and Gluten-Free Options:
