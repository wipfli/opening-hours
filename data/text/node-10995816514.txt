ABOUT US
Family-run at its core, mi-tê focuses on bringing contemporary Vietnamese street food culture to Zurich.
Our cuisine is inspired by tradition, elements of Eurasian fusion, and the creativity of our team in the kitchen. But there's more.
With our Vietnamese coffees, we aim to introduce Switzerland to the little-known world of Vietnamese robusta coffees, offering a whole new way of experiencing coffee.
Discover the fresh and authentic aromas of Vietnam's streets at mi-tê, where a warm welcome awaits you.
