About this location
Kitchen Reunion in the Mall of Switzerland (Ebikon) is your destination for culinary diversity. We unite three strong concepts under one roof, ensuring that all your shopping wishes are fulfilled: Thai Wok Kitchen , Guapo Mexicano , and Jack's Burger .
What you can expect from us:
Thai Wok Kitchen: Authentic woks, curries & noodle dishes – freshly prepared right before your eyes.
Guapo Mexicano: Spicy tacos & burritos – uncomplicated, fresh and full of flavor.
Jack's Burger: Juicy premium burgers & comfort food – the perfect pick-me-up for your shopping break.
In short: Choose variety, order quickly and enjoy fresh – #justorder .
