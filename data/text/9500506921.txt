We visited at around 10pm and appreciated finding somewhere still open and serving food that late. The young guy serving us was also very friendly. Unfortunately, the food was extremely disappointing for the price. The chicken vindaloo contained only two pieces of chicken, with the rest mainly consisting of potatoes. The chicken was chewy, the curry was barely spicy, and I left around half of it. The poppadoms were also very dry, although they did remove these from the bill. Nobody checked whether we were enjoying our food during the meal; we were only asked at the very end. We also ordered a hot chocolate for CHF 7 and were given a glass of warm milk with a sachet to mix into it ourselves. The food might have been acceptable if it were inexpensive, but considering how much they charge, it was extremely poor value. We wouldn’t return.
