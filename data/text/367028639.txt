Restaurant Lugano
The restaurant overlooks the enchanting Gulf of Lugano, a place where natural beauty and style come together to create the perfect atmosphere for special moments: a business lunch, a romantic date, or dinner with your closest friends.
Under the guidance of Chef Emanuele Bertelli, the cuisine at Ristorante Seven Lugano begins with a meticulous selection of ingredients, with particular attention to seasonality and provenance. Traditional Italian dishes, appetizers, first courses, meat and fish main courses are given a modern twist and international influences, with taste and good taste. Among the specialties, the Grosse Pièce dishes to share stand out, such as sea bass in a salt crust or chateaubriand.
Sushi and sashimi lovers will appreciate the creations of our Sushi Master Chef: delicate traditional flavors and surprising fusion combinations for those seeking gastronomic adventures.
For lunch and dinner, the Seven Lugano Restaurant is the ideal place for a unique gastronomic experience.
Opening: December 2014
