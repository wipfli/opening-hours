Located on Stampfenbachstrasse 68 in Kreis 6, close to Zürich HB, ALBA Stampfenbach brings our sourdough craft to the city center. In the evening we shift back to our Neapolitan style pizzas available for takeout and delivery. Please note that we do not accept reservations. To pre-order your pizza for delivery or takeout, follow the order link above. We only accept credit card payments. Cash is not accepted.
