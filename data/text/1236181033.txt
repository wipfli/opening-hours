The Pinch
Where the tastes of Santa Monica intersect with the ingredients on a Melbourne plate and a number of palates in between
A cafe, restaurant and bar open non-stop daily from breakfast through to late night with a warm kitchen all day long.  Convenience, friendly service and great food are at our core.
We offer some old time favourites along with dishes you never thought you’d try — but glad you did.   Our offerings continue to evolve with new dishes we create from our own imaginations and memories from our travels, along with cooking up some suggestions that you may have for us as well
We are passionate about innovation and creativity and we promise to work harder, stay up later, find ways to say ‘yes’ to special requests you may have.  So if it’s breakfast for dinner that you’re up for, we are too….  We’re breaking some rules and think you’ll enjoy your time with us even more as we do.
