Description
Welcome to L'Atelier Grandsire!
Our exclusive pâtisserie boutique, vinothek and restaurant has a beautiful, French-style ambience. Our delicious, high-quality products are made in limited numbers with lots of passion and attention to detail.
Come by and discover our wide range of culinary delights:
- Breakfast and Brunch
- French pastry
- Lunch
- Varied wine selection by glass or bottle with suitable Snacks
- Dinner in a relaxed atmosphere
Enjoy the freedom to decide if you would like to discover our chef's surprising menu with a matching wine accompaniment. Or to choose by your own, out of our à la carte dinner menu where we serve all our dishes in small portions in a fine dining style.
Are you looking for a special and unique gift?
L'Atelier Grandsire supports Swiss artists by offering them a platform to exhibit and sell their artworks.
Our chalet consists three floors:
- The main restaurant in the basement : cozy and discreet with its stunning clima-controlled wine cabinet
- The pastry boutique at the ground floor: flooded with light and modern with its half-height and high tables
- The mezzanine : under the gable, you can enjoy a very special ambience. Its a perfect privat space up to 12 guests
We are looking forward to welcome you!
