Welcome To Saravanaa Bhavan
Welcome to Saravanaa Bhavan - a culinary haven where tradition meets taste! Discover the essence of South Indian cuisine in our brand new restaurant at Limmastrasse 50 in the heart of Zurich.
Vegetarian delights Immerse yourself in a world of vegetarian delights, meticulously prepared with the freshest ingredients and authentic South Indian spices. From spicy curries to crispy dosas, our menu offers a symphony of flavours to tantalise your taste buds.
