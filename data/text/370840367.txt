BOUNJOUR CHEZ LOUIS & TINA
Characterized by French influences, Brasserie Louis appears in Art Deco style mixed with modern elements. The terrace offers a first-class view of the pulsating life in the Niederdorf. Next to the Brasserie is the Louis Take-Away, above it the legendary Tina Bar.
We are looking forward to your visit!

– TINA BAR –
THE LEGENDARY BAR IN THE NIEDERDORF
The Tina Bar is located on the second floor of the Brasserie Louis. It is legendary and cult beyond the city limits and offers space for 40 people. Every full-blooded citizen of Zurich knows the Tina Bar. It often serves as a “backdrop” for film and photo shoots. The Tina Bar is also a fumoir and the exquisite selection of cigars and drinks makes the choice a torture.
