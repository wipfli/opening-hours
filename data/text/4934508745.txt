About KLE
KLE is a small, welcoming restaurant in the heart of Zurich, inspired by flavors from around the world.
We cook with care and intention, letting ingredients speak for themselves. Our table is a place to relax, connect, and enjoy food in a calm and inviting setting.
We look forward to hosting you soon.
Menu
Guided by the seasons and close relationships with local farmers, our vegetable-forward menu changes throughout the year, following the rhythm of the land.
You can choose a 4 to 6-course surprise menu with optional alcoholic, non-alcoholic pairings or drinks a la carte.
