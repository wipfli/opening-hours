ALBA WIEDIKON PIZZERIA
Bremgartnerstrasse 70, 8003 Zurich
Nestled amid the lively Kreis 3 in the heart of Wiedikon, ALBA is a modern, Italian-inspired and locally interpreted pizzeria with a focus on sourdough. Wiedikon is the first home and location of ALBA’s sourdough pizza. This location is only open during dinner service and also offers takeout. While we always keep a few tables available for walk-ins, we highly recommend making a reservation in advance to ensure we can accommodate you. Follow the link below to reserve your table and secure your spot. We are happy to reserve the table for you for a time window of two hours. Please be aware that we do not accept cash payments, credit cards only.
‍
ALBA Wiedikon has two different rooms and atmospheres. The Sunrise room, with its lively and Mediterranean energy; and the Sunset room, with its more intimate and subdued feel, offer two very distinct experiences. While we can't guarantee which part of the room you will be seated in, we will try our best to accommodate your request if you mention it while placing your reservation.
