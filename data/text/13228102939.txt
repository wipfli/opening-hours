Centrally located in the city of Lausanne, is our new adventure.
Lausanne is a city in Kanton Vaud on Lake Geneva, also known as the home to the International Olympic Committee headquarters, the Olympic Museum and lakeshore Olympic Park. Restaurant is located in the heart of the city, close to the Saint-François Galeries. You can reach us by 7 minutes’ walk from Lausanne train station or simply take metro to Bessières.
Restaurant has 80 seats in house and provide services such as Takeaway and deliveries. Guests can use our online system on our website or simply go to justeat, ubereats or smood to order. No worries, if you do not find us in your area, just call us, we will cater to you. Restaurant has a small hall of 25 seats, suitable for business lunches and family events.
Above all, our warm team is always ready to welcome you with a smile to make you feel at home.
