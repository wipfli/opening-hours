Enjoy your visit to the Restaurant Lötschberg and discover the wealth of Switzerland’s culinary offerings. Over 200 wines, traditional artisanal PDO and PGI products from sustainable agriculture, plus a wide range of regional specialties. All served in an informal atmosphere, or to take home. We will be delighted to advise you and look forward to welcoming you.

PDO-IGP products
The creation of PDO and PGI products requires traditional methods, a place of origin, people and their artisanal skills. Only a limited number of Swiss products are produced in this way and bear the protected PDO and PGI quality labels.

LABEL FAIT MAISON
Here you can enjoy authentic, high-quality food that meets the requirements of the Fait Maison label.
The Label “Fait Maison” is awarded on a voluntary basis to restaurants whose dishes are prepared entirely or for the most part in their own kitchens, without recourse to pre-cooked dishes and products.
