Palafitte Hotel - Neuchâtel, Switzerland
Welcome to Hôtel Palafitte, on the shores of Lake Neuchâtel
From the unique comfort of our stilted pavilions, each moment invites you to slow down, breathe, and savour. Whether on foot or along scenic trails or around an exceptional table — every experience unfolds at your own pace.
Between lake, forest and mountain, discover a refuge where energy and ease live in harmony. Hike or rest, dine or daydream — here, your stay is yours to create.
Hôtel Palafitte invites you to embrace the season — simply, fully, freely.
