Restaurant Guarda Val is evolving
From 11 October 2026, we will be investing in the future of our restaurant.
After more than 16 years of continuous year-round operation, Restaurant Guarda Val will close for approximately eight weeks to undergo its first major transformation. During this time, we will be redesigning the guest areas and creating a new chapter in the story of Guarda Val.
With great attention to detail, we are investing in the atmosphere, comfort and overall guest experience – not because what exists today is no longer appreciated, but because we believe our guests deserve to be inspired and delighted for many years to come.
The foundations of our cuisine remain unchanged: exceptional products, precise craftsmanship and genuine hospitality. At the same time, we are embracing a new culinary vision.
We are bringing the world to Sporz.
Inspired by flavours and traditions from all four corners of the world, we combine international influences with outstanding ingredients, refined craftsmanship and the unique atmosphere of our historic mountain hamlet.
This is not about serving international cuisine for its own sake. It is about creating a culinary journey where the world meets Sporz.
Going forward, the focus will be even more on the joy of dining itself – whether you choose a small discovery, a single signature dish or a longer culinary experience. The number of courses will no longer define your evening; instead, you will be free to enjoy exactly as you wish.
During the renovation
Throughout the renovation period, our Bündner Stube Crap Naros will welcome you every day for breakfast and dinner.
Breakfast will continue to be served daily, while in the evening you can enjoy regional classics and seasonal specialities in the warm and authentic atmosphere of our Bündner Stube.
Wine lovers need not miss out either: our complete wine selection from the Guarda Val cellar will remain available throughout the renovation.
Our popular Grandes Pièces – including the classic Chateaubriand and whole fish carved tableside – will also continue to be available by advance reservation.
We look forward to welcoming you from early December 2026 to a restaurant that honours its heritage while opening the door to a new world of hospitality, generosity and unforgettable culinary experiences.
