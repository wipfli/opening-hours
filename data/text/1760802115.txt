Welcome to Kennedy’s
Kennedy’s pub in Zurich is not just a home from home for Zurich’s Ex-Pat community, it’s the place to be for anyone looking to make new friends, whilst enjoying home-made food & great beer!
Our friendly international team will make you feel at home from the moment you step into our pub. Pass the time by checking out the hundreds of unique antiques & artifacts that make Kennedy’s such an interesting & welcoming place.
Come join us for the CRAIC, we’re just a stone’s thrown from Zurich main station.
FOODS & DRINKS
Are you hot enough to survive our hot & spicy chipotle Burger? Hungry enough to take our Beef & Guinness pie?
Come & try our delicious home-made food, it’s a decision you won’t regret!
