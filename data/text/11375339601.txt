HOUSE-MADE NOODLES
Artisan Noodles, Made with Precision.
Our desire to create the best ramen noodle possible led to us importing a Yamato noodle machine all the way from Japan. With painstaking attention to detail, we use a precise blend of Japanese and Swiss flours to handcraft each noodle with love. The result? A perfect ramen noodle, ready to be slurped and to carry that amazing broth.

Our flagship Lausanne Restaurant
Step into our Lausanne restaurant, meticulously designed by artisan joiners and leading architect Pierre Winthrop and Partners.
