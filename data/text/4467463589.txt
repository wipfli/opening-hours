Experience the enchanting Bernese Jura with us
The personally decorated inn in the Jura country house style from the 18th century is furnished with great attention to detail. The Mont-Soleil's oasis of tranquillity is an exceptionally good place to relax. After sporting activities, unwind with a glass of wine in the library or on the cosy garden terrace. We welcome you to our little paradise!
It is a beautiful auberge with its authentic and rustic charm in the countryside, quiet in the middle of nature.
The kitchen is traditional such as ham with rösti (jambon à l'os),  Rossini with morel sauce, beef tartare.
We work with the products of the region, cheese fondue Tête de Moine, sausage, local dried meat, local cheese and many other artisanal products, veal meat from our own farm (farm of the Assesseur), beef and pork, potatoes from the region, etc.
We are convinced that our kitchen will be appeal to locals, as well as tourists.
We are carring to our dear customers.
You are welcome to enjoy our homemade cocktail and discover the new menu of the Assessor.
