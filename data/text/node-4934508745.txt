ABOUT KLE
KLE, stemming from the german name "Sauerklee" refers to a plant that has been used throughout most civilizations of the world for millennia.
We offer a plant based menu in a welcoming and vibrant dining room using local produce as our canvas.
Looking at the world as a source of inspiration, we share our love for food from our little
neighbourhood restaurant.
