ABOUT US
Restaurant New Bombay in Zurich
Our Indian restaurant with take-away is located in a quiet place in the old town of Zurich, in the Niederdorf at the Predigerkirche. With 60 seats inside and another 60 seats on the terrace, we are also the right place for larger groups for an Indian evening. Treat yourself to a little break and enjoy quiet moments with Indian food in a historic building in one of the most beautiful and yet central places of Zurich.
