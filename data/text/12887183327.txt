ABOUT US
BaoBae is a modern Chinese restaurant near Lochergut in Zurich, founded by Chef Dan and May Shu. We serve creative Chinese sharing plates, a seasonal tasting menu, Asian-inspired cocktails and a curated wine selection. Guests can dine indoors, on our courtyard terrace or reserve a separate section for group celebrations.
BaoBae means “treasure” or “darling” in Chinese, a fitting name for our husband-and-wife venture rooted in a shared love of food and hospitality. Drawing on Dan’s extensive experience at restaurants including Michelin recognized Gaijin Izakaya, Old Inn, Tsukasa and Chifa Izakaya, our cuisine celebrates China’s diverse regional flavors through a modern lens. From fiery Sichuan influences to delicate Cantonese notes, each dish reflects Chef Dan’s roots, creativity and personal interpretation of Chinese food.

THE BAOBAE MENU
At BaoBae, every plate tells a story and every ingredient has a purpose. Discover modern Chinese sharing plates and Asian tapas inspired by regional flavours, traditional street food and Chef Dan’s childhood memories. From our signature peanut noodles to seasonal creations designed for sharing, the menu is bold, playful and distinctly BaoBae.

THE DRINKS
Complement your meal with inventive drinks on tap by the infamous Marco Colelli (Best Bartender World Class Switzerland 2025 | Liquid creator | Ora bar) and John Oquendo (Liquid creator) at one of Zurich’s coolest cocktail bars, right here in our vibrant asian bar. A distinct and thoughtful wine list, formulated by our Chef de Service Paul Waldschuetz and Stefano Petta (Director of wine - The Living Circle | Switzerland's best sommelier | Gault Millau Sommelier of the Year 2024), featuring rare “Dragon” wines and fun modern winemakers, drink what you love or follow our Somm’s guidance. Wines or Cocktails, the drinks selection just might be better than the food! Our signature crafted cocktails that fuse asian ingredients with mixology artistry, make it the perfect spot for evening vibes or a night out.
