Hotel Restaurant Seegarten Marina Spiez
Located directly on the lake, nestled in the picturesque bay of Spiez on the beautiful Lake Thun, surrounded by a magnificent mountain landscape, you will find genuine hospitality with us in the beautiful Bernese Oberland.
We look forward to welcoming you and wish you many individual moments of leisure and enjoyment with us.
The M. & M. Beutler family, owners
Caroline Reber & Juri Seidel, managing directors
and the entire Seegarten Marina team
Welcome to the most beautiful bay in Europe!
Discover the nearby town and the surrounding nature, enjoy the lake, stroll along the nearby vineyard, explore Spiez Castle or simply enjoy the wonderfully fresh air and the view.
