¡Bienvenidos a Barranco!
Let us take you on a gastronomic journey.
Our dishes taste best when shared – much in the spirit of the laid-back quarter Barranco, where life is centered around the community, optimism and good food.
We believe in focus on quality of the produce we use, a strong attention to detail, and a welcoming, down to earth approach to hospitality. We use a blend of authentic Peruvian and South American products and Swiss ingredients to create a dynamic, exciting menu. In the glass we offer cocktails with our own twist, and mostly biodynamic wine from small family-owned winemakers.
Our team is diverse from many different backgrounds, all with a passion for gastronomy.  We pride ourselves in our community.
We hope you join us on this gastronomic journey.

Summer Al Fresco
Soak up the evening sun and the neighborhood's atmosphere on our cozy terrace, right overlooking beautiful Bullingerplatz. Perfect for long summer nights or for an apéro.
