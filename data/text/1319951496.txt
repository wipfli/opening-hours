001 / OUR STORY
A Breton tradition,
our own way,
in the heart of Lausanne
Since 1983, La Chandeleur has carried on the art of the crêpe and the galette in the heart of Lausanne's old town. Tucked away on the picturesque Rue de la Mercerie, a stone's throw from the cathedral, our crêperie has become a must for lovers of Breton cuisine.
True to the essentials — the genuine buckwheat galette, the wheat-flour crêpe, dry or sweet cider — we also love to wander off the beaten track. Over the years we have developed our own creations, freer and more contemporary: original pairings that bear our signature. Breton at heart, yet resolutely our own.
