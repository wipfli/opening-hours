WÖLLKOMM
The Appenzeller Huus brings together the two established houses, Huus Löwen and Huus Bären. With Huus Quell, a sanctuary has emerged that combines timeless elegance, natural serenity, and mindfulness. Here, traditional craftsmanship meets modern luxury – surrounded by the inspiring landscape of Appenzell and shaped by a culture that values authenticity.
HUUS BÄREN 3*
WÖLLKOMM AT 3* SUPERIOR HOTEL BÄREN
Shaped by its origin, inspired by the future and inspired by the moment - the heart of the APPENZELLER HUUS.
HUUS QUELL 5*
WÖLLKOMM AT 5* SUPERIOR HOTEL QUELL
Your hideaway for relaxation on a new level awaits you: 2200 square meters of wellness in an idyllic landscape.
HUUS LÖWEN 4*
WÖLLKOMM AT 4* SUPERIOR HOTEL LÖWEN
HUUS LÖWEN combines Appenzell tradition with the latest hotel standards. In addition to its sun terrace and Löwenstobe lounge, this boutique hotel also offers plenty of space for events of all kinds.
WELLNESS
Indulge in holistic moments of wellbeing at the Quell Spa of the Appenzeller Huus. Spanning over 2,200 square metres, this serene world is shaped by natural elements, gentle light, and pure spring water from our own source. Feel the soothing power of the Nature Zones, immerse yourself in herbal aromas and soft light, and let body and mind find harmony. Signature treatments invite you to experience beauty and relaxation in perfect balance. Your spa journey finds its gentle finale at the rooftop spa with breathtaking views of the Säntis mountain.

GASTRONOMY
Our wide range of offerings reflects the diversity of Appenzell. Traditional, rustic, regional, vegan, or vegetarian, the Appenzeller Huus offers enjoyment at the highest level.
BÄRENSTOBE
RESTAURANT
The Bärenstobe stands for elevated dining in a historic setting – deeply rooted in the region and thoughtfully modern.
LEHNERSTOBE
RESTAURANT
The Lehnerstobe complements the Bärenstobe and offers a uniquely welcoming ambiance with a cozy fireplace and artistic wallpapers by Jakob Schlaepfer.
CIGAR LOUNGE
BAR & LOUNGE
For cigar lovers, our cigar lounge in the 17th-century Schmitte offers the right ambiance to enjoy their treats in peace.
TAVERN
BAR & LOUNGE
The tavern is the meeting point for our guests and visitors. In the daytime, you can have coffee or a snack, or later, a cozy aperitif by the fireplace.
BOTANICUM BAR
BAR & LOUNGE
At the BOTANICUM BAR, classic cocktails are reimagined with local herbs and fruits – authentic, creative, and in harmony with the spirit of the APPENZELLER HUUS.
RESTAURANT QUELL
RESTAURANT
At Restaurant Quell, modern brasserie culture, Swiss hospitality and creative cuisine come together. Executive Chef Michael Gollenz shapes a culinary style full of character – vibrant, contemporary and guided by a refined sense of composition and flavour.
WINE CELLAR
WINE
Nestled within the APPENZELLER HUUS lies one of Switzerland’s most remarkable wine cellars. Cellar master Hans Rhyner invites you to wine tastings – exclusively in the ‘wine barrel’ for up to nine people, surrounded by the cellar atmosphere.
PRIVATE DINING
RESTAURANT
Fine dining becomes an exceptional experience in the wine barrel: an exclusive, atmospheric retreat for private events, small celebrations, or intimate moments of enjoyment. The rustic wood, warm atmosphere, and cuisine from HUUS QUELL create a unique setting for unforgettable hours in private company. Reservations available upon request for up to 10 people.
LÖWENSTOBE
RESTAURANT
Traditional Appenzell cuisine fused with modern, sustainable and sometimes vegetarian or vegan recipes, amidst beautiful ambience and floral art.
APPENZELLERSTÖBLI
RESTAURANT
The Appenzellerstöbli used to be the village bakery. It offers traditional cuisine in a time-honored ambiance. Chef Michael Gollenz reinterprets traditional Appenzell specialties.
JAKOB SCHLAEPFER LOUNGE
BAR & LOUNGE
Directly next to the Löwenstube is the Jakob Schlaepfer Lounge - the artist's wallpaper design exudes elegant charm and bright colors.
SUMMER TERRACE
RESTAURANT
Enjoy summer and Green Egg specialties in the shade of the trees, surrounded by the scent of our in-house herb garden and with a view of the Alpstein.
