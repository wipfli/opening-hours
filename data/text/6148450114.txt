WINSTON CHURCHILL
Grilling meat on a hot stone is a special experience. This traditional preparation comes from Argentina. The Steakhouse Ochsen was one of the first restaurants in Switzerland to offer this unusual preparation of meat for many years and is therefore well known. Vegetarians also have the opportunity to try this specialty with delicious stuffed mushrooms.
