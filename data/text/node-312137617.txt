Our menu
Cantina is a modern Latin American eatery where friends and family come together to eat, drink and celebrate.
At Cantina we use a blend of authentic Latin American products and Swiss ingredients to create a hearty, soulful, exciting menu. In the glass we offer homebrewed chicha, cocktails, and European, mostly biodynamic wine.
