Weissenstein restaurant
DAILY INFORMATION
Marcus Seidel and his team are all about fresh, local produce. We source our products from local producers wherever possible. Look forward to a mixture of classics and unique homemade creations.
The Weissenstein à la carte restaurant
Boasting panoramic views of Solothurn and the Alps, our à la carte restaurant offers a truly special experience that makes every meal unforgettable. Within the restaurant, vast windows allow you to savour the far-reaching vistas come rain or shine.

DAILY MENU
Self-service
Our dining options also include a self-service restaurant on pleasant days, weekends and holidays. From 9:00 am to 4:00 pm, you’ll find snacks to reenergise you before your hike, ice cream for the little ones and a range of other convenient options.
Breathtaking views
On sunny days, we invite you to enjoy our à la carte menu on the terrace. Take in the stunning scenery stretching from the Swiss Plateau to the Swiss Alps, all while indulging in our delectable cuisine. To avoid waiting times, we recommend booking a table.
BOOK A TABLE
The finest wines
To go with our dishes, we have selected over 100 wines for you and stored them in our wine cellar. Our focus is on Swiss wines, which we supplement with offerings from the surrounding countries. For environmental reasons, we do not offer New World wines.
