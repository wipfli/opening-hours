ABOUT US
Maharani Restaurant
Maharani Restaurant At Maharani Restaurant Bern, we bring the royal taste of India to Switzerland. Our dishes are prepared with traditional recipes, fresh ingredients, and aromatic spices that reflect the true essence of Indian cuisine. Whether you're dining in with family, ordering takeaway, or celebrating an occasion, we ensure warm hospitality and delicious food every time.
Authentic Indian Cuisine
Enjoy homemade curries, fresh tandoori breads, biryanis, and vegetarian & non-vegetarian specialties inspired by the diverse flavors of India.
Qualified Chefs
Our experienced chefs prepare every dish with precision, quality, and love—giving you a memorable food experience.
