What began as a small, brave step has grown into something steady and strong. Since taking over an established restaurant in Wetzikon, Rumarasa has evolved quietly but consistently, shaping its own identity, refining its rhythm, and building something that feels truly ours. A place with history became our home, and we grew into it day by day. Today, we’re not only part of the neighborhood; we’ve become a name people know, trust, and return to. Our kitchen is rooted in Indonesian flavors. Warm, direct, and honest. Interpreted in a way that feels natural here in Switzerland. No show, no noise, just clarity, balance, and a sense of home in every plate. We’re growing, we’re learning, and we stay close to our roots. And just as we are, we welcome you in.
