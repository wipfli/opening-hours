An iconic hotel since 1865
Beau-Rivage Genève, an extraordinary place, your timeless retreat. With its exceptional location facing Geneva’s jet d’eau and firmly anchored both in the past and present, Beau-Rivage Genève is one of Geneva’s landmark luxury hotels, and its 52 rooms and 43 suites with their remarkable style ensure that you have a unique experience in the heart of this global city. Welcome to the elegance and sophistication of a period building, its yesteryear charm passionately preserved, for over 160 years.

Culinary experience
Beau-Rivage is a true haven for food lovers. Whether you’re enjoying the warm, elegant setting of Rivage Café, sharing delicious tapas-style plates, savoring a leisurely brunch, or indulging in a refined fine dining experience, Chef Mathieu Croze and his team are here to make every meal unforgettable, from breakfast through dinner.

Rooms and Suites
A haven of charm and elegance, each room and suite at Beau-Rivage Genève is a world of its own. From intimate rooms to exceptional suites ranging from 30 m² to 190 m², every detail has been thoughtfully designed to offer a unique experience.
